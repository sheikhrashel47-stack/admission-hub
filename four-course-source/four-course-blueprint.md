## Coverage map

**Degree of Comparison** covers degree identity, formation and spelling, positive degree, comparative degree, superlative degree, interchange, advanced comparison, and the master trap/revision zone. The source includes 40 micro-rules, 18 admission traps, and the 70-question bank. High-priority patterns include `as...as`, `than any other`, `than all/most other`, `one of the`, Latin comparatives with `to`, `much` vs `very`, logical comparison with `that of/those of`, absolute adjectives, and the two-person comparative rule.

**Subject–Verb Agreement** covers foundation/core noun detection, indefinite pronouns, tricky nouns, compound subjects and proximity, of-phrases, unit nouns, relative pronouns, and advanced traps. The source includes 50+ rules, 12 admission traps, 35 micro-rules, and 76 MCQs. High-priority patterns include `each/every/neither`, `news/mathematics/furniture/information`, `police/cattle`, `a number of/the number of`, money/time/distance as units, `one of the boys who` vs `the only one of the boys who`, `more than one`, and here/there inversion.

**Same Word, Different Parts of Speech** covers function-first classification, noun/verb conversion, adjective/adverb roles, preposition/conjunction/adverb distinctions, multi-role words (`like`, `that`, `only`, `enough`, `back`), determiner/pronoun boundaries, context-based strategy, and trap revision. The source includes 30 micro-rules and 50 MCQs. High-priority patterns include position and function tests, `hard/hardly`, `late/lately`, `before/after/since/until`, relative `that` vs conjunction `that`, `enough`, `round`, `home`, and participle adjectives.

**Right Form of Verb** covers verb forms, tense mapping, subject–verb agreement, infinitive/gerund, modal and special forms, conditional/subjunctive structures, error detection and context, and the final trap/revision zone. The HTML guide includes 60 admission-style MCQs and repeated rules for V1/V2/V3/V-ing, time signals, modal forms, `used to` vs `be used to`, preposition + gerund, if-clauses, wish/it is time, and context-based correction.

## Implementation plan

Each course uses 8 lessons with 5 visual slides per lesson. Visuals are deterministic in the shared renderer and include formula cards, flow maps, comparison panels, rule tables, trap boards, score bands, and master maps. Source questions remain tagged by course and grouped by difficulty family; the existing Exam Zone, instant feedback, bookmark, note, edit, duplicate, delete, and Result Sheet features are reused without changing the global Question Bank.

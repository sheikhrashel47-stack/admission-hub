১. Number (বচন ব্যবস্থা)

গণনাযোগ্য বিশেষ্যের পরিমাণগত দ্বৈত রূপান্তর:

  • Regular: -s, -es, -ies, -ves (ছাদ-ক্লিফ-চিফ ব্যতিক্রম)
  • Mutation: man->men, goose->geese, louse->lice
  • Foreign Latin: -us->-i, -um->-a, -a->-ae, -ex/-ix->-ices
  • Foreign Greek: -is->-es, -on->-a (criteria, phenomena)
  • Compounds: passers-by, sons-in-law, spoonfuls
  • Always Singular: advice, furniture, news, summons
  • Always Plural: scissors, spectacles, assets, alms
  • Invariant: sheep, deer, salmon, aircraft, species
  • Differentiated: brethren/brothers, clothes/cloths

২. Gender (লিঙ্গ ব্যবস্থা)

প্রাকৃতিক জীবতাত্ত্বিক লিঙ্গভিত্তিক চতুর্মুখী বিভাজন:

  • Masculine: পুরুষ (King, Lad, Drone, Stallion, Drake)
  • Feminine: নারী (Queen, Lass, Bee, Mare, Duck)
  • Common: উভয়লিঙ্গ (Sovereign, Spouse, Sibling, Baby)
  • Neuter: জড়বস্তু ও একক সমষ্টি (Table, Pen, Committee unit)
  • Heteronyms: wizard/witch, hart/hind, ram/ewe
  • Foreign Suffixes: executor/executrix, hero/heroine
  • Personification: Sun/Death (Masc) vs Moon/Peace/Ship (Fem)
  • Defective Gender: blonde, brunette, spinster, widower

৩. বচন ও ক্রিয়া সঙ্গতির গোল্ডেন ফর্মুলা (Syntactic Concord Radar)

The number of + Plural Noun
= SINGULAR VERB (is / has)
The number of candidates is high.
A number of + Plural Noun
= PLURAL VERB (are / have)
A number of candidates are waiting.
One of the + Plural Noun
= SINGULAR VERB (is / was)
One of my friends is an engineer.
One of the + Plural Noun + Who
= PLURAL VERB (are / have)
He is one of the boys who have passed.
Many a + Singular Noun
= SINGULAR VERB (has / was)
Many a student has failed the test.
A pair of + Plural Noun
= SINGULAR VERB (is / was)
A pair of trousers was bought.
